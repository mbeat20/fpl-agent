# Creator Stance is one record with two storage facets, not two separate concepts

Layer 3 lists creator recommendations under the structured database and creator opinions under the vector database, which could be read as two different things. It's one concept: a Creator Stance's structured fields (player, buy/sell, confidence, reason, creator) live in the structured DB; its source raw text is chunked and embedded in the vector DB, linked back by reference. Structured queries ("what does creator X think about player Y") hit the structured table directly; semantic queries ("find opinions about Newcastle's fixture run") hit the vector DB and resolve back to the same Stance for its structured fields. This avoids the two stores drifting out of sync, at the cost of every Stance write touching both stores. A single piece of raw content (one video, one thread) can still produce multiple Stances, each pointing at the same or overlapping vector chunks — the link is many-Stances-to-one-source, not strictly 1:1.
